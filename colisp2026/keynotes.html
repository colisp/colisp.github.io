專題演講一

Shigeo Sugimoto -
An Overview of the Japan Library Association (日本図書館協会) and a Personal Perspective on Its Future Shape

任職單位:
• Professor Emeritus, University of Tsukuba
• Executive Director, Japan Library Association
演講摘要:
The Japan Library Association (JLA) was established in 1890 as 日本文庫協会 and renamed 日本図書館協会 in 1908. After World War II, JLA was re-established as an incorporated association in 1947 and became a public interest incorporated association in 2012. JLA has long served the library community in Japan as a key organization. However, since the turn of the century, it has faced a decline in both individual and institutional membership.
Like ALA and other organizations, JLA has Divisions and Committees through which members participate in its activities. In 2025, JLA established an ad hoc committee to examine organizational issues concerning its Divisions and Committees, with the aim of making JLA more attractive to current and prospective members.
The ad hoc committee has agreed that Divisions and Committees are essential to JLA. Through discussions and surveys, however, it has also identified the following issues:
- Divisions and Committees tend to work in silos;
- their activities are not well understood by JLA members; and
- JLA needs a new organizational unit to support voluntary activities based on interests shared by members across domains and regions.
This talk first provides an overview of the history and organizational structure of JLA and then presents the issues discussed by the ad hoc committee.

專題演講二

Emi Ishita -
Research Data Management and Open Science Initiatives at Kyushu University in the Era of Digital Transformation

任職單位:
• Professor, Kyushu University
演講摘要:
As part of its digital transformation (DX) strategy, Kyushu University established the Institute for Data-Driven Innovation Initiative in April 2022 to promote digital transformation across the university. Within the institute, the Research Data Service Division was established to support research data management and to advance Open Science through the proper management, preservation, and dissemination of research data.
The division provides a comprehensive range of services, including research data storage infrastructure, consultation on Data Management Plans (DMPs), educational resources, and support for the publication and sharing of research data. In addition, in collaboration with the Department of Library Science, the division offers a certification program for research data management support professionals, aiming to develop human resources capable of supporting research data management and Open Science initiatives. These efforts contribute to strengthening research integrity, enhancing the reusability and long-term value of research data, and helping researchers meet the evolving expectations of funding agencies and the broader scholarly community.
This presentation introduces the establishment and activities of the Research Data Service Division at Kyushu University and discusses its contributions to institutional digital transformation. It also highlights practical experiences and lessons learned in promoting research data management and Open Science at a large research-intensive university.

專題演講三

宋育泰 — AI 成為資訊入口之後：中央社 MCP 想解決什麼問題？

引言人：謝吉隆(國立臺灣大學新聞研究所副教授兼所長)

任職單位:
• 中央社資訊暨創新中心主任
演講摘要:
本場次由國立臺灣大學新聞研究所謝吉隆所長擔任引言人，說明本屆年會安排此場演講的緣由，以及新聞媒體數位創新與圖書資訊服務之間的關聯。 隨後由中央通訊社宋育泰資訊暨創新中心主任分享中央社MCP的開發歷程，以及新聞媒體在零點擊時代的因應做法。
有一天，AI 會變成人們取得資訊的第一道門。大家把問題丟給聊天機器人，取得答案就走，原本會點進新聞網站的讀者，從社群時代流失一點、到零點擊的時代消失了。 與此同時，伺服器熱鬧異常，每 5 千次爬蟲抓取，只帶回一位真正的讀者。讀者選擇 AI 圖的是便利，但不代表不在乎信任。
圖書館與新聞媒體一樣，是現代社會資訊、知識的重要堡壘，面對沒有人再進網站的未來，中央社回顧自身、嘗試走到讀者和 AI 代理（agent）所在之處。 中央社成立百年，一路累積下來的新聞稿件、現場照片、時事彙整，是關於現代台灣最詳盡的紀錄，保存台灣自己的用語和價值觀。而對國外讀者（與他的 Agent ）來說，中央社 MCP 是一扇不脫離脈絡、看見台灣多元面貌的窗。
宋主任演講結束後，由謝吉隆所長將中央社的實務經驗延伸至圖書資訊領域，探討圖書館發展AI服務的可能方向。
